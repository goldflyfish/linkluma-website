LinkLuma features

Network status, made clear.

LinkLuma keeps current traffic and connection details within reach on your Windows 11 desktop.

Made for a quick look

The essentials, easy to read.

Live activity and connection health each have their own place, so one number never has to tell the whole story.

↓ ↑

Live traffic

Watch current download and upload speeds in clearly labeled units. These readings show traffic now, separate from a speed test.

◎

Two clear statuses

See whether your local network is connected and whether Windows reports internet access. Checking and unavailable states are shown plainly.

▣

Your kind of widget

Choose a full widget or a compact mini view. LinkLuma stays in the system tray when you close the floating widget.

⌁

Recent traffic graph

Open a graph to follow recent download and upload activity over time, with missing readings shown as gaps.

▤

Data usage

Review downloaded, uploaded, and total traffic for the selected adapter in hourly, daily, and monthly views.

◷

Connection history

Review recent observed connection changes and monitoring gaps alongside the current status.
